PASS if the message offers to skip the questions and just start, and asks one open
question (a single question may have two clauses, like "what do they get, and who is
reading it?") about a concrete moment, person, or use. Briefly noting what it would build
on if they skip is fine. FAIL if it asks several separate questions, gives a list of
lettered or numbered options, stacks three or more questions into one sentence, or starts proposing a design.
